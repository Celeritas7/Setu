# Evening brainstorm spots: what I learned (2026-10-18, Saitama)

**Goal:** a quiet place to brainstorm at night. It should have food, be cheap and let me stay a long time.

## Places
| Place | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Jonathan's Higashi-Urawa | Meal only (~1 hr), **not** a work session | Table sign says 2+ people per table and no long study, PC or iPad work. |
| Food courts / supermarket eat-in areas (Musashi-Urawa) | Too early for night use | They close around 21:00–22:00. |
| Net cafés (private booths) | **Best fit for long solo work** | The only places built for long solo stays. |
| Work-friendly cafés | Worth trying | Long solo stays are normal there. |

## Jonathan's prices
- Drink bar: **¥439 incl. tax** (menu No. 360). Must be ordered with food.
- Lunch drink bar: ¥296, weekdays 10:30–17:00 only.

## Rules of thumb
- Laptop and study rules differ by branch. Check for a table sign before settling in.
- Pick places where staying long alone is normal. Avoid places where it stands out.
- **No beef.** Check menus for 牛肉 / ビーフ. Avoid hamburg steak (ハンバーグ) and meat sauce (ミートソース).
